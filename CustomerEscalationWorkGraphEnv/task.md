# Customer Escalation Case CE-001

A customer has contacted support claiming they were charged twice for an order, while the order system still shows the order as unpaid.

The organization must investigate the case and determine the correct remediation.

Different specialists are responsible for investigating:

- payment transaction evidence
- order state
- customer support history

The specialists should independently inspect their relevant evidence.

Their findings must then be combined before a final remediation decision is made.

Do not modify the source evidence files.
